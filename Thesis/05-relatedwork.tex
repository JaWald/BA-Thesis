\chapter{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}
The impact of \ac{DOP} principles on execution time has been investigated in prior studies. In 2021, Nyberg \cite{nyberg2021dod} tested four aspects of \ac{DOP} for performance and cache utilization: removing polymorphism, switching from non-contiguous to contiguous structs, reducing struct size and contrasting a cache-friendly \ac{OOP} layout to an \ac{ECS}. All four improved performance and lowered miss rates for the data and unified caches, except for the removal of polymorphism, where the cache miss rate rose slightly.

While Nyberg isolated the individual effects, Faryabi \cite{faryabi2018dataoriented} in 2018 examined whether they would materialize in practice. In one of three experiments, he converted the Unity game DwarfHeim to an \ac{ECS}, though the conversion was not purely \ac{DOP}. Performance increased in some cases, but Faryabi warned the gains could not be directly attributed to \ac{DOP}, as too many confounders remained.

In 2020, Fedoseev \cite{fedoseev2020application} likewise studied \ac{DOP} in practice, comparing two nearly identical mobile games built with \ac{OOP} and \ac{DOP} across performance, maintainability and required skill level. The \ac{DOP} build's frame rate was roughly \qty{10}{percent} higher and maintainability was equal, though code reuse in follow-up projects was higher. On skill level, he noted \ac{DOP} might require a solid background in computer science, as it demands comprehension of computer architecture and memory.

In 2022, Wingqvist et al. \cite{wingqvist2022evaluating} compared four builds of a small game: an \ac{OOP} and a \ac{DOP} build, each in single- and multi-threaded variants. Testing object counts of \num{5000} to \num{100000} with cache miss rates simulated via \texttt{Valgrind}, the results favoured \ac{DOP}, reaching a maximum speedup of \num{13.25} with the multi-threaded variant and a cache miss rate reduction of \num{5.57}. The authors note, however, that a real game seldom features more than \num{5000} objects, so practical speedups would likely be smaller.

While these all focus on the impact of \ac{DOP} on video games through practical comparisons, none isolate the differences across the full memory layout spectrum of \ac{PC}, \ac{AoS} and \ac{SoA} in Rust, as done in this thesis.
